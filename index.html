How do I get my Instagram export?
  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Request a download. Choose Format: JSON (HTML also works), date range All time.
  3. You can limit it to Followers and following to get a smaller file faster.
  4. When the email arrives, download the ZIP. On iPhone it saves to Files (or your Downloads).
  5. Tap the box above, choose Browse → find the instagram-….zip in Files, and select it. No need to unzip — even a large export works, only the followers/following parts are read.
